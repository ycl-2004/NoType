# NoType 0.5.0

NoType 0.5.0 makes R2T2, a streaming speech model, the only local engine: it transcribes while you
speak, so the transcript arrives about a second after you stop. It adds hotwords for names and
technical terms, Esc to cancel, and a simpler voice overlay. Speech remains on the Mac.

## What's new in 0.5.0

- **R2T2 Streaming** (NetEase Youdao's Confucius4-R2T2, through llama.cpp and Metal) is the default
  and only local engine. It handles mixed Chinese and English and transcribes during recording.
- **Hotwords**: Engine → Edit Hotwords… opens a plain text file, one term per line. Distinctive
  names and terms are recognised more reliably, especially in 中文优先 mode.
- **Esc cancels** a dictation while recording or transcribing, without inserting text or touching
  the clipboard, and without the Esc reaching the app you are typing in.
- The **voice overlay** shows only the waveform while recording, or the waveform and the newest live
  text, and sizes itself to its content.
- Punctuation where long recordings are split no longer leaves stray `。 ` or `，。`.
- Launch no longer spends about 35 seconds re-verifying the model, and quitting no longer crashes.

## Upgrading from 0.4.0

- Qwen3-ASR and SenseVoice were removed. A saved choice of either loads R2T2, which downloads about
  1.5 GB on first launch.
- Their old model folders in `~/Documents/huggingface/models/k2-fsa/` are not removed
  automatically; delete them if you no longer need them.
- Replacing an ad-hoc signed app can reset Microphone and Accessibility permissions; grant them
  again if dictation does not start.

## Download and install

1. Download `NoType-0.5.0-arm64.zip` and unzip it.
2. Move `NoType.app` to `/Applications`, replacing an older copy if present.
3. On first launch, Control-click the app and choose **Open** if macOS asks for confirmation.
4. Grant Microphone and Accessibility permissions when requested.

The archive does not contain speech models. NoType downloads R2T2 from a pinned Hugging Face
revision on first launch, checks it against the publisher's SHA-256 hashes, and reuses it across
app updates.

## Requirements

- Apple Silicon Mac (`arm64`)
- macOS 15.0 or newer; macOS 26 or newer for the optional macOS Speech engine
- About 2 GB of free space for the app and the R2T2 model
- Microphone permission for recording
- Accessibility permission for shortcuts, target capture, and text insertion
